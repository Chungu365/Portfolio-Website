About me

Financial judgement and technical build, in the same pair of hands.

Most analytical work fails in one of two places. The numbers are right but nobody can act on them, or the story is clear but the model underneath will not hold. I work across both, bringing valuation and financial statement discipline to the analysis, and proper data modelling to the build.

That means a dashboard whose measures reconcile, a valuation whose assumptions are visible and switchable, and a written recommendation a finance lead or a board can move on without a translation layer. I am based in Lusaka and work with organisations wherever the data is.
