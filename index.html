About

Financial judgement and technical build, in the same pair of hands.

Most analytical work fails in one of two places: the numbers are right but nobody can act on them, or the story is clear but the model underneath won't hold. I work across both — bringing valuation and financial-statement discipline to the analysis, and proper data modelling to the build.

That means a dashboard whose measures reconcile, a valuation whose assumptions are visible and switchable, and a written recommendation that a finance lead or a board can move on without a translation layer. I'm based in Lusaka and work with organisations wherever the data is.
